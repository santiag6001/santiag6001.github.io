📋 What this platform does

Many people find it hard to manage AI models. This platform simplifies the process. It handles the heavy lifting so you can focus on building your specific project. You get a controlled environment for testing, checking accuracy, and serving your models.

  • Experiment Tracking: Save your progress to compare different model settings.
  • Evaluation: Measure how well your AI answers questions.
  • RAG System: Connect your own documents to an AI for better, more accurate answers.
  • Ready Serving: Run your models in a way that other software can talk to them.

🛠️ Running Your First Experiment

Once you open the software, the main dashboard shows your active dashboard. You can create a new experiment by clicking the button labeled "New Project." Give your project a name and define the parameters you wish to test. The system will guide you through the process of pointing it to your local data files.

If you want to use the smart search feature, place your documents in the designated folder. The system reads these files and makes them ready for the AI. You can then ask questions based on these documents.

📊 Monitoring Performance

The platform includes built-in tools to watch how your AI performs over time. These graphs show you if changes to your settings make the model smarter or slower. Use this data to refine your model until it meets your requirements.

How can I see errors? If something stops working, check the logs tab in the sidebar. This page lists events in plain text so you can see what happened.
